ACCT 5105

Financial Reporting & Assurance in a Blockchain World. Seven Monday sessions, 6:15–9:15 p.m., Bauer Room BH130. You design a testnet coin, put it into circulation, and say what an assurer can stand behind.

Open the 7 modules Syllabus Setup Handbook

How to follow

Each module is one Monday. Modules 1–6 have two parts. Module 7 is demo day and does not split.

WhenWhat you do
Before the sessionRead the two session pages. Jupyter before Module 1, a wallet before Module 1’s second part, Remix before Module 3. Publish an address. Never a seed.
6:15–7:17 p.m.First part. Run every Demo, read Keep this straight, then fill YOUR TURN.
7:30–8:32 p.m.Second part. Same pattern. The buffer until 9:15 p.m. is for the quiz, Remix, or help.
After the sessionThe module gate, and a lab in Modules 2, 4, and 6. Due at 9:15 p.m. that Monday unless the page says otherwise.
Final projectA team of 1, 2, or 3. Sign up on Canvas under People → Final project teams before Module 3 (Nov 9). One memo and one live defense.

Quizzes are closed notes and stay on Canvas. This site has the student pages and notebooks only. It has no quiz questions and no solutions. Testnet only. No mainnet, no real money, and no public sale.

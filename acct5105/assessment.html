ACCT 5105-01 - Financial Reporting & Assurance in a Blockchain World

What you will turn in

This course is one project. You design a community money, practice it on the class testnet (Modules 3-6), and on Dec 14 you show the product live: memo + on-chain proofs for a licensed firm. The PDFs capture the design. The clicks are the course.

After Module 2 we stay on testnet and we practice every week. Words first (Modules 1-2). Then Remix, MetaMask, Sepolia, explorer, registry. Green Demo / Do this now means you click with me. Sitting and watching does not fill the Evidence Passport.

What you leave with. Not a slogan about blockchain. A packet you could show in an interview: how the chain works in detail, what an assurer can vouch, and how this shows up in finance (reporting, controls, product, go / partner / pass).

If a word feels hard: open Start here first (no finance or blockchain required). Keep the semester cheatsheet open all term. Then re-run that session’s Demo cells, read Keep this straight, then open handbook.html (cafeteria / house-key / lemonade pictures). Each class has its own page under sessions/ (S1-S13) plus a practice notebook. Every class notebook starts with Words we use today and ends with Check you understand. If you can explain the picture to a sharp 15-year-old, you understand it. If you can only say “decentralized,” open Start here before you write.

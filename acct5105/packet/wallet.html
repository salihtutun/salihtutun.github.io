ACCT 5105-01 - Financial Reporting & Assurance in a Blockchain World

Open a wallet in 15 minutes

Done when you can paste your 0x address into a notes app and you can say the network name on the screen (default: Sepolia, unless the board says otherwise). Then stop.

You do not need CLASSUSD or faucet ETH for this check. Those come in Session 2 after your address is on the roster.

MetaMask is the usual choice. Rabby or another EVM browser wallet is fine. Phone-only wallets are harder to use with Remix later.

1. Browser

Use Chrome, Brave, Firefox, or Edge on your laptop. You will need this same browser in Session 6.

2. Install from the official site

Open metamask.io/download (or your wallet’s own site). Add the extension.

Do not install from a random Google ad, an email, or a Discord link.

3. Create a wallet — seed on paper, then close that screen

Choose Create a new wallet. Write the secret recovery phrase (seed) on paper. Put the paper away.

Then close the seed screen. You should never see those words on a projector, in a screenshot, or in a chat.

If you already have a wallet, you may use it. Still never paste the seed.

4. Copy the address

In the wallet, copy the account address. It starts with 0x and is safe to share. That is a locker number.

Paste it into a notes file on your laptop so Session 2 is a copy-paste, not a scramble.

5. Name the network

Look at the network dropdown at the top of the wallet.

  • Ethereum Mainnet is the wrong country. We never use it in this course.
  • Add or select Sepolia unless the instructor’s board says a different class testnet.

You do not need to click a faucet yet. The one faucet link will be in Canvas before Session 2.

If it breaks

What you seeTry
Two MetaMask icons / “wrong account”You installed twice. Use one browser, one extension
Address looks like a sentence of wordsThat is the seed. Close it. Copy the 0x locker number instead
Network says EthereumSwitch to Sepolia (or the name on the board)
“I need CLASSUSD”Wait. Session 2 drips it after the roster has your address. Do not invent a contract address

Do not send a seed, a seed screenshot, or a private key to anyone — including the instructor.

ACCT 5105-01 - Financial Reporting & Assurance in a Blockchain World

Open Remix in 15 minutes

Peek (before Session 5). Remix opens and the OpenZeppelin ERC-20 wizard shows mintable / pausable / cap. Remix’s own fake chain (Remix VM) is enough. You have not clicked Deploy on Sepolia or any public network.

Live (Session 6). Injected Provider, network name matches the board, then deploy in the room. Do not deploy at home the night before.

You are not writing Solidity from scratch.

1. Open Remix

In the same browser as your wallet, go to remix.ethereum.org.

Allow it to run. Close the intro tour when you can see the file explorer.

2. Open the OpenZeppelin wizard

Either:

  • in Remix, open the OpenZeppelin Contracts Wizard, or
  • open wizard.openzeppelin.com and use Copy to Remix

Pick ERC-20. Tick:

  • Mintable
  • Pausable
  • Burnable
  • Premint / cap so there is a cap (the box lid from A2)

Name and symbol wait for your A2 table. For this check, placeholders are fine.

3. Peek only (Session 5)

Environment: Remix VM (fake chain). You may compile. You may deploy on that fake chain if the instructor does a peek.

Do not switch to Injected Provider and deploy to Ethereum Mainnet. Mainnet for this course is a zero on the project.

Do not deploy CLASSUSD. That is the instructor’s coin.

4. Live deploy is Session 6

In class:

  1. Cap math in the Session 6 notebook must fit before you click Deploy.
  2. Environment: Injected Provider (MetaMask).
  3. Network in the wallet = the name on the board (default Sepolia).
  4. Deploy. Copy the contract address onto the class registry. Never invent one.

If Remix or the faucet dies: finish the notebook YOUR TURN (cap math) and write the failed transaction in the A3 runbook. That paragraph is points.

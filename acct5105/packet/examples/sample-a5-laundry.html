SAMPLE A5 — SpinHour claim + netting (not Eagecoin)

This is a SAMPLE. It is not Eagecoin. Steal the honesty about “if we disappear.” Use your Week 4 trades if you have them. If the class has no cycle, label the triangle instructor example (this page already does).

Working title: SpinHour · ticker SPIN

Part 1 — What the coin is

  1. The thing, plus a promise. 1 SPIN is 15 minutes of washer/dryer time the RA team still owes. It is not a coat-check stub for a pile of quarters in a drawer. If we had stored cash and issued receipts, we would say so.
  2. Redemption. Any listed RA honors a booked slot: burn 2 SPIN, start a 30-minute timer, the machine is free. Within 24 hours of the booked time, or we post the fail and refund in CLASSUSD from treasury.
  3. If the team disappears. Holders have a grade problem and an empty laundry room, not a court claim. We say that out loud. CLASSUSD in their wallet is still the instructor’s chip, not ours to seize.
  4. Atomic vs fail. A pool swap is both legs or neither (Session 9 Demo). “I sent 2 SPIN, you left a water bottle on the washer” is not atomic — one ledger moved, the machine did not. That is why the in-room redemption is a photo and a burn.

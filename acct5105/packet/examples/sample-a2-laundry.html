Sample — steal the shape, not the community

SAMPLE A2 : Dorm laundry issuance (SpinHour)

This is a SAMPLE. It is not Eagecoin and not your submission. Steal the table shape and the honesty. Invent your own community (must match your A1).

Working title: SpinHour · ticker SPIN

Part 1 : Rules (sample answers)

  1. Minter : The two RAs named on the floor roster (team EOAs). If both RA keys are lost, minting stops until Housing names a replacement RA in writing; we do not put a seed in this PDF.
  2. Cap : 800 SPIN (about two weeks of evening slots). Premint in Remix = 800.
  3. Burn / redeem : Burn when a booked washer/dryer slot starts (RA marks start). That retires the 15-minute promise.
  4. Pause : Either RA may pause transfers if a machine is down, a key may be leaked, or a joke-gone-wrong flood of gifts. Written trigger goes in the RA group chat log.
  5. Decimals : 0 (whole tickets). Nobody needs fractional minutes for a dryer.

Part 2 : Who gets coins (sample)

Primary method: airdrop (gift). Not a sale for CLASSUSD, not a wage for folding clothes.

  • 12 residents on the floor roster × 20 SPIN each at the start = 240
  • Pool reserve (TEAM + CLASSUSD bowl later): 200
  • Treasury (RA ops / missed-slot cushion): 200
  • Any roster classmate may hold and transfer; guests are out (A1).
  • Why not sale / earn: a gift into mailboxes is not a ticket booth (no CLASSUSD in) and not a paycheck (no work invoice). Selling SPIN with a “10×” pitch would change the legal object : we refuse that story.

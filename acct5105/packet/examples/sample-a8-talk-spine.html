Backup line (first 15 seconds if MetaMask dies)

“Wallet is down. We are on the circulation log and explorer history from the registry — same four proofs.”

Business close (optional if asked)

“This course trains three Monday-morning questions: what can we vouch, is this a unit/rail/claim, and go/partner/pass with kill rules. Laundry was the practice case.”

Cold Q practice (do not memorize laundry answers as your coin)

  • Explorer proves balance/burn; does not prove the dryer heated.
  • Wrong-network zero balance → check network + registry before remint.
  • Kill: 24 hours; buy booking list, ignore public-chain token for laundry.

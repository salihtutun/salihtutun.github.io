Cold questions (2 minutes — be ready)

Instructor (or peer) may ask any of these. Answers must match your passport and memo:

  1. What does this explorer page prove — and what does it not prove?
  2. Point to the stranger holder. How do we know they are not on your team?
  3. State your kill number. What fires if it trips?
  4. Buy vs ignore: one stack piece the firm should not copy.
  5. If a teammate cannot explain a sentence from A7 heading 5 or 7, that sentence is treated as not yours.

Room rules

  • Testnet explorer or Remix. No mainnet.
  • No seed phrases on the projector.
  • Paste addresses from the class registry — never invent a live 0x on the projector.
  • Do not read a ChatGPT script. Notes for your hashes are fine; generic “blockchain is transparent” cards are not.
  • Sit down at 8:00.

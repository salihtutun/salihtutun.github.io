Assignment

A8 — Demo day (Session 13)

In one sentence. Prove the coin circulated, then recommend go / partner / pass. Slides do not save a team with no hashes.

WhenSession 13
WhoTeam. Every teammate speaks at least once.
Time8 minutes + 2 minutes of questions
Points40% of the course

Backup. If MetaMask dies, say so in the first 15 seconds and open the circulation log + explorer history. Hidden “we will skip the live part” after minute 6 cuts the proof score in half.

Stuck? Handbook → Session 13 (five proofs in order). Do not read A7 aloud.

Live proofs — this order, then talk

  1. Contract — network, address, cap, who may mint/pause
  2. A classmate not on your team holding the coin (balance on screen)
  3. Pool quote — TEAM / CLASSUSD, refresh live
  4. A transfer and a redemption — tx hash (or photo of minutes/cookie delivered)

Then, and only then, talk.

Talk spine (stay inside 8 minutes)

MinutesWhat
0:30The problem
1:30Who mints, what the claim is
3:00The four live proofs above
1:00What broke (one specific story)
1:00Go, partner, or pass — one verb

One optional slide of parameters is enough. We score circulation and judgment, not polish.

Room rules

  • Testnet explorer or Remix. No mainnet.
  • No seed phrases on the projector.

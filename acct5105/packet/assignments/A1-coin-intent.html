Assignment

A1 — Coin Intent (Module 1)

In one sentence. On one page, name the club, the problem, and what one coin stands for. No code.

This session after class. Open a blank page. Put names at the top. Copy the six headings below. Fill each in plain words. Export PDF. That is the whole assignment.

DueEnd of Module 1
WhoTeam of 1, 2, or 3
Turn inOne PDF, max 1 page, 11 pt. Names and emails at the top. Working title + ticker (3–5 letters).
Points10 (counts toward the 19% module gates)

Stuck? Re-run the Session 1 Demo cells (jobs, ledger, T-account). Still fuzzy? Handbook → Session 1. If a sentence uses “blockchain” as the reason the coin exists, rewrite it. Need to see the shape of a one-pager? Read the SAMPLE laundry time-bank — steal the headings, not the dorm story.

You will not

  • write code or draw ticker art
  • need a wallet yet
  • design a coin that only your friends may hold (unless you explain an allowlist)
  • treat price speculation as the main job

You will (use these exact headings)

  1. Community — Who is in? Who is out? How does someone join?
  2. Problem — One sentence. A payment or coordination problem — not “we want a blockchain.”
  3. Unit of account — Do people think in CLASSUSD, in your coin, or in a real good (hours, coffees)? Why?
  4. What the coin is — Treasure for the holder and a promise by whom?
  5. Supply idea — Who may create units, what caps them, what retires them? (Draft only; A2 locks this.)
  6. How classmates will use it by Module 4 — One concrete trade (example: 30 minutes of tutoring).

Pick a shape from Session 1 if you need one: time-bank, campus voucher, Sarafu-style local money, SME trade credit, or loyalty.

If you cannot name the community and the problem, you do not get a token in Module 3. That is the whole assignment.

Blockchain technology you must understand in detail

Business judgment without the tech is empty; tech without judgment is a pitch deck. By demo day you must explain each item in class words, tied to your project:

#TechnologyYou must be able to say
1HashFingerprint of data; change the input → different hash. Integrity, not secrecy.
2Signature + address vs seedSignature proves authorization; address is public; seed never on the PDF or projector.
3Block + prev_hashA page linked to the prior page’s sticker; quiet edit breaks the chain.
4Nonce · difficulty · PoWGuess counter + beep-test of work — not “the bank encrypts money.”
5Verify / tamper evidenceWhat fails first after a quiet edit — in your words.
6Consensus vs registrarGlobal agreement vs one trusted list-keeper; you rented Sepolia’s consensus.
7Smart contract (ERC-20)Posted house rules: mint, cap, pause; contract address is the asset you vouch.
8Custody / control of keysWallet vs exchange tab vs fund share (three tickets).
9What the chain proves / does notBalances and burns vs off-chain performance (dryer heated, tutoring delivered).
10UnbundleBuy vs ignore: list vs chain, registrar vs consensus, token vs escrow.

If you cannot explain #1–#10 aloud on demo day, you are not done — regardless of slide polish.

Guiding questions (under each heading)

1. Problem and community — Whose problem? What is broken today? Why would a firm care Monday?

2. Mechanism — Who may mint / pause, and what is the cap? What does one unit redeem? If the banker key is lost, what happens (roles — never a seed)?

3. Circulation evidence — Which explorer pages / hashes can an assurer vouch? Who is the stranger holder? What does the chain not prove?

4. Market — Spot vs average and depth in your numbers? Who traded? What risk language (not a listing pitch)?

5. Unbundle (new writing) — Chain vs shared list? Consensus vs rented Sepolia? Token vs escrow? Buy vs ignore: one piece the firm should not copy.

6. What broke — One dated ops / control failure only your team lived. What would you put in a risk memo?

7. Go / partner / pass — One verb · one A6/L3 kill number · one compliance line · why a firm can act Monday.

8. Sources — At least three: (i) babysitting co-op or Sarafu, (ii) one Naranjo result, (iii) one MIT or BIS idea.

Use these eight headings (guiding questions under each)

1. The problem and the community

  • Whose coordination or payment problem is this (name the community)?
  • What is broken today without a shared ledger?
  • Why would a licensed firm care about this problem on Monday?

2. The mechanism

  • Who may mint / pause, and what is the cap (from your A2 table)?
  • What does one unit redeem or claim in the real world?
  • If the banker key is lost, what happens (roles — never a seed)?

3. Circulation evidence

  • Which explorer pages / tx hashes can an assurer open and vouch?
  • Who is the stranger holder (classmate not on your team)?
  • What does the chain not prove (performance / delivery)?

4. What the market revealed

  • What did spot vs average and depth look like in your A4/L2 numbers?
  • Who actually traded — and was the book thin?
  • What risk language would you use (not a listing pitch)?

5. Unbundle the stack (new writing)

  • Did you need a chain, or would a shared list + registrar have been enough?
  • Did you need consensus, or did you rent Sepolia’s?
  • Did you need a token, or would escrow have fixed the fail?
  • Buy vs ignore: one stack piece the firm should not copy.

6. What broke

  • One dated ops / control failure only your team lived (A3–A6).
  • What would you put in a risk memo so it does not happen twice?

7. Go / partner / pass

  • One verb only.
  • One A6/L3 kill number (what fires if it trips?).
  • One compliance line (license / Howey / Travel Rule — your A6 Part 2).
  • Why a firm can act on this Monday — not a slogan.

8. Sources

At least three: (i) babysitting co-op or Sarafu, (ii) one Naranjo result (PoW / AMM / peg), (iii) one MIT or BIS idea. Handbook Sources list. A blog recap is not enough.

Rules

  • Audience: Head of Product + CAO / engagement partner. No hype. No price prediction.
  • Numbers beat adjectives. Business judgment must be usable Monday morning.
  • You may not paste ChatGPT (or any model) drafts of headings 5 or 7. Steal shape from the SAMPLE only.
  • Teammates may share logs; the unbundle and recommendation must be the team’s own words (one memo per team).
  • A6 + L3 were due Fri Dec 11 so this memo can cite them. Do not invent kill rules on demo morning.
  • If a sentence is in the PDF, every teammate must be ready to explain it aloud on A8.

This is not a whitepaper and not a pitch deck. It is advice a licensed firm can act on — built from your team’s define → design → circulate → decide arc.

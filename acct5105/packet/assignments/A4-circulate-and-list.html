Hard rules

  • Do not put a seed phrase or private key in the PDF or the sheet.
  • Paste contract addresses from the class registry. Never invent a 0x.
  • One teammate’s five txs do not count for the whole team. Each student owns Part 1.

Go together (before you grade yourself)

  1. MetaMask → Network: Sepolia.
  2. Remix → Environment: Injected Provider - MetaMask → At Address with your registry contract.
  3. Read balanceOf in Remix. Match it on Sepolia Etherscan.
  4. Then pay (Part 1) and seed the bowl (Part 2).

Part 1: Payments (Session 7)

Each student (not just one teammate) completes:

  • At least 5 peer transfers (mix of your TEAM coin and CLASSUSD)
  • At least 1 transfer that pays for a posted class good (tutoring, notes, a voucher)

Unit vs rail on every row. Write both:

  • Unit: how you think the price (usually CLASSUSD on the tag)
  • Rail: what actually moved (TEAM transfer, CLASSUSD transfer, or shadow-book label)

Log every row: date, from, to, token, amount, unit price, tx hash (or shadow book), what was purchased.

Failed txs count. Wrong amount, insufficient balance, wrong network: paste the hash (or note “no hash / wallet died”) and one line on what you meant to do. Planned honesty beats silent panic.

Defined good. Seller posts the good and the unit price before you pay. Buyer settles on an agreed rail. Hash goes on the shared sheet now, not later.

Part 2: Market (Session 8)

  1. Finish lab L2 (spot, gulp, IL language).
  2. Seed a TEAM / CLASSUSD pool (testnet Uniswap or the class notebook bowl). Write which.
  3. Post starting piles x, y, and k = x \times y. Implied listing: CLASSUSD per TEAM = y / x.
  4. Show one swap by a classmate who is not on your team. Log who, direction, size, hash (or shadow-book flag).

Spot is pool geometry (next tiny sip). Average is what that gulp cost. Do not treat them as the same number. The class bowl has no fees. The IL gap is not a trading fee.

Market note (1 page PDF)

Must include:

  • Starting piles and k = x \times y
  • Price after that external swap (new spot; average paid if you can show it)
  • You are the LP (you filled the bowl). One paragraph: what impermanent loss means for you on this trade
  • Would you hold TEAM overnight vs CLASSUSD? One honest sentence

“Share in the system” means hashes in the log, not a slogan on a slide.

Evidence pack (what I open when I grade)

  • Shared sheet: ≥5 peer rows per student, with unit + rail + hash (or labeled shadow book)
  • ≥1 defined-good payment row
  • One stranger / external swap row
  • 1-page market note PDF with x,y,k, post-swap spot, IL paragraph, overnight sentence
  • Optional: one failed-tx row with a one-line fix story (passport loves this)

This would fail (do not write this)

  • “People will trade it” with no hashes
  • Only one teammate’s five txs for the whole team
  • A live quote you invented (copy piles from the bowl, or label example)
  • Spot and average treated as the same number
  • Calling the IL gap a trading fee
  • Deploying a second coin because Remix felt lonely
